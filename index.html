Biography

Khloe Kim is a data engineer and MLOps engineer completing a B.S. in Internet of Things at SoonChunHyang University. She is moving from manufacturing analytics into production ML systems, building the connective layer between messy operational data and reliable AI, from SQL analysis and reporting to Kafka ingestion, model retraining, and RAG-based agent workflows.

Her recent work includes a semiconductor defect-prediction platform built on an 11,939-wafer SK hynix dataset, a legal consultation multi-agent system with labor-domain RAG reaching 93% in-scope accuracy, and Chicky, an emotion-aware AI companion doll. Before that, she owned production-loss and station-bottleneck reporting at HDI, a U.S. Samsung-device refurbishment operation, grounding her engineering work in real operating constraints.
